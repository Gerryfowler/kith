Getting started

Who should I add first?

Start with your inner circle — the three to five people you'd drop everything for — then the friends you're actively trying to get closer to ("Close"). Everyone else can wait in "Friendly".

How do I log a conversation?

Open Log, tap the box, and either type or use the microphone key on your keyboard. Say who you saw, who suggested it, and roughly how deep it went — Samvar works out the rest and shows you a draft to check before saving.

Why isn't someone showing as overdue?

Each circle has a rhythm: Inner about weekly, Close every three weeks, Friendly every two months. Someone only appears in "Reach out today" once they've gone quiet longer than that. Newly added people get a grace period.

Nudges & openers

Nudges aren't arriving

Check You → Nudges isn't set to Off, and that iOS Settings → Samvar → Notifications is allowed. Nudges are scheduled on your phone at the time you chose.

The opener didn't mention anything specific

Openers draw on the facts stored for that person. Log a few conversations with details ("she's training for a half marathon in March") and they'll get better. The personalised versions are part of Samvar Pro.

Your data

Moving to a new phone

You → Your data → Download backup on the old phone, then Import backup on the new one. Samvar has no cloud copy, so this file is the only way to move your history.

Deleting everything

You → Erase all data removes everything from the device immediately. Deleting the app does the same.

Subscriptions

Managing or cancelling Samvar Pro

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Samvar. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Restoring a purchase

You → Samvar Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

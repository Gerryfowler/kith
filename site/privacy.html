Samvar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Applies to the Samvar iOS app and samvar.app

Samvar is built around a simple idea: the people in your life are yours, not ours. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information, in plain language.

What Samvar stores, and where

The names, circles, notes, facts, phone numbers, addresses and interaction history you enter are stored only on your device. Samvar has no user accounts and no server-side copy of your data. If you delete the app, that data is gone unless you exported a backup file first. Backups are ordinary files you control.

What leaves your device

  • AI features (Samvar Pro). When you use "Review & score" on a note or "Opener" on a person, the text of that note and the facts you have stored about the people involved are sent over an encrypted connection to Samvar's server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to be understood or drafted, and returns the result. Samvar's server does not store the content of these requests. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms, which do not permit training on this data. If you are not a Pro subscriber and use the simple built-in rules instead, nothing is sent.
  • Places. If you type an address for a person or use "Near me", the address or your approximate coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find a location. Results are cached on your device. You can use Samvar fully without ever adding an address.
  • Contacts. If you choose to import from Contacts, Samvar reads only the entries you pick, and only names, phone numbers and addresses. They are stored on your device and never uploaded.
  • Messages. "Send" hands a drafted message to the Messages or WhatsApp app on your phone. Samvar does not send messages itself and cannot read your conversations.
  • Notifications. Nudges are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

What Samvar does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing or renting of your information to anyone.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No account, email address or password required.

Subscriptions

Samvar Pro is purchased through Apple. Apple handles payment and provides Samvar only with an anonymous receipt confirming the subscription is active. We never see your card details.

Children

Samvar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you already have complete access to it, can export it at any time (You → Your data → Download backup) and can erase it (You → Erase all data). If you have used Pro features and want to ask about any request logs, or have any other question, email privacy@samvar.app. Users in the UK and EU have the rights set out in UK GDPR and GDPR, including the right to complain to the ICO or your local supervisory authority.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you on launch and the date at the top of this page will be updated.

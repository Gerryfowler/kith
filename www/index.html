How scoring works

Every conversation earns smiles: how (in person 10 · call 5 · message 2) × type (quick catch-up ×2.5 · quality time ×5). It doesn’t matter who reached out — every conversation counts the same. Each person’s smiles then fade a little every day — faster for closer circles — so the score genuinely slips when you go quiet. The headline score blends how many of your people are in rhythm (55%), the share of quality time (25%) and how much you talk overall (20%).

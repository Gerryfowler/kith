Smart understanding (Claude)

Paste your Anthropic API key and Kith uses Claude to genuinely understand your notes — several interactions in one ramble, indirect phrasing, relative dates. The key is stored only on this device and is never included in backups. Without a key, Kith falls back to simple keyword rules.

How scoring works

Each interaction earns points: channel (in-person 10 · video 6 · call 5 · message 2) × depth (transactional ×1 · social ×2 · substantive ×4 · deep ×8) × initiation (you ×1.5 · mutual ×1.2 · them ×1). Every person’s balance then decays daily — faster for closer circles — so the score genuinely goes backwards when you go quiet. The headline score blends circle coverage (50%), depth mix (20%), initiation (15%) and volume (15%).

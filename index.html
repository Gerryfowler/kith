Appearance

Smart understanding (Claude)

Paste your Anthropic API key and Kith uses Claude to genuinely understand your notes — several interactions in one ramble, indirect phrasing, relative dates. The key is stored only on this device and is never included in backups. Without a key, Kith falls back to simple keyword rules.

Birthdays → your Calendar

Every birthday Kith knows (🎂 facts) can become a yearly event in your iPhone Calendar with a 9am reminder — native alerts without Kith needing notification permissions. Open the downloaded file and tap Add All.

Places

Contacts imported with an address can be placed on the map, powering “near me” and place search on the Network tab. Lookups go to OpenStreetMap (one per second, each address only once, cached on your phone).

How scoring works

Each interaction earns points: channel (in-person 10 · video 6 · call 5 · message 2) × richness (logistical ×1 · friendly ×2.5 · substantive ×5) × initiation (you ×1.5 · mutual ×1.2 · them ×1). Every person’s balance then decays daily — faster for closer circles — so the score genuinely goes backwards when you go quiet. The headline score blends circle coverage (50%), depth mix (20%), initiation (15%) and volume (15%).

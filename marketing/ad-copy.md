## Reddit (text-forward)

**Title:** I built an app that tells me which friend I've gone quiet on, and writes the first text
**Body:** Three circles (Inner, Close, Friendly), each with a rhythm. Every morning it names one person, reminds me what we last talked about, and drafts the opener. Everything stays on the phone. Free to start, iPhone only for now. Would love honest feedback from people with full lives.

## Founder post (LinkedIn / Facebook, organic, launch day)

I went quiet on some of the people I love most. Not on purpose — a busy year became three. So I built Samvar: it treats friendships like fitness. Three circles with their own rhythm, a score that slips when you go quiet, one person to reach out to each morning, and the first line written for you from what you actually know about them. It's on the App Store today, free to start. If you've got a friend you keep meaning to call, this is for you. [App Store link]
